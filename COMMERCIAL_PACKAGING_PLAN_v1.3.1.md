# AI Employee v1.3.1 Commercial Packaging Plan

## Purpose

Define the commercial model for transforming AI Employee from a production-certified platform into a scalable SaaS product.

## Product Packaging Strategy

AI Employee will be delivered through three commercial editions:

## 1. Customer Edition

Target:
- Small and medium businesses
- Online stores
- Service companies
- Teams needing AI employees

Core capabilities:
- AI Employee workspace
- Customer conversations
- Knowledge base
- Basic automation
- Reports dashboard

Suggested plans:

### Starter
- 1 AI Employee
- Limited conversations
- Basic knowledge storage
- Single workspace

### Business
- Multiple AI Employees
- Advanced automation
- More memory capacity
- Team access

### Professional
- Unlimited workflows
- Advanced analytics
- Priority support

---

## 2. Reseller Edition

Target:
- Agencies
- Consultants
- SaaS operators
- Technology partners

Capabilities:
- Multi customer management
- Client workspaces
- Branding options
- Usage monitoring
- Reseller dashboard

Revenue model:
- Monthly reseller subscription
- Revenue share
- White-label upgrade

---

## 3. Vendor Edition

Target:
- Enterprise providers
- Platform owners
- Large organizations

Capabilities:
- Full tenant management
- Developer API
- Custom integrations
- Enterprise security controls
- Dedicated deployments

---
