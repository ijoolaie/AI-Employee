# AI Employee SaaS Monetization Model v1.3.1

## Goal
Define the commercial foundation for turning AI Employee into a recurring SaaS product.

## Target Customers
- Small businesses needing AI support employees
- Online shops
- Service companies
- Enterprise teams

## Pricing Model

### Starter
- Single tenant
- Limited AI employees
- Basic knowledge memory

### Professional
- Multiple AI employees
- Workflow automation
- Analytics
- Integrations

### Enterprise
- Dedicated deployment
- Advanced RBAC
- SLA
- Custom integrations

## Revenue Streams
- Monthly subscription
- Usage based AI consumption
- Enterprise licensing
- Marketplace extensions
- Professional services

## SaaS Metrics
- MRR
- ARR
- Churn
- Activation rate
- AI employee usage
- Customer lifetime value

## v1.3.1 Objective
Create the minimum commercial layer required for selling AI Employee as a service.
